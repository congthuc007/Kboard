# Kboard v1.1.12 Release Notes

## Toi Uu & Tu Dong Dong Bo IP Khi 2 May Chung Mang Wi-Fi

### 1. Tu Dong Ket Noi May Nhan Trong Cung Mang LAN
- **Tu dong ket noi**: Khi 2 may cung ket noi vao mot mang Wi-Fi (hoac Hotspot), may ban phim khi quet thay may nhan duy nhat se tu dong ket noi voi may nhan tim thay.
- **Hien thi dia chi IP ro rang**:
  - Giao dien thanh ket noi tren may ban phim hien thi ca 2 dia chi:
    `May nay: <IP_May_Ban_Phim> -> <IP_May_Nhan>:8964 [Doi]`
  - Giup nguoi dung quan sat 2 may trong dai IP mang Wi-Fi noi bo (vi du may ban phim la `192.168.1.20`, may nhan la `192.168.1.15`).

### 2. Canh Bao Lech Lop Mang (Subnet Matching)
- Truong hop may ban phim va may nhan ket noi vao 2 mang khac nhau:
  - Thanh cong cu se hien thi nhan canh bao mau vang: `Khac dai IP • Hay [Quet LAN]`.
  - Giup nguoi dung nhan biet su bat dong bo mang de ket noi lai cung mot Wi-Fi.

### 3. Tich Hop Nut Bat Tat Nhanh Hotspot
- Khi may ban phim nhan dien dang ket noi vao dai phat song di dong (`192.168.43.x`), he thong hien nut `[Dung Hotspot]` de chuyen ve `192.168.43.1:8964`.
